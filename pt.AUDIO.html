AudioVision’s HiFi Show – Oct 17th & 18th

By Brian Hunter

September 22, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 3:47
Instaread

Although the city of San Francisco lacks any independent audio show for 2026, a few times a year the audiophile faithful get the call to coalesce together at AudioVisionSF in the heart of the city by the bay. The annual event this year is dubbed “Welcome to the HiFi Show” and is taking place on October 17th and 18th.

The tagline might be a clever bit of marketing, however, HiFi shows in the area do tend to be a bit lacking, especially considering the buying power and relatively enthusiasm locals tend to display in the region – and that isn’t even taking into consideration the massive DIY groups that thrive alongside the new purchase market.

In that regard, AudioVision has become something of a reliable gathering point for Bay Area listeners. Owners Antonio Long and Randolph Johnson have repeatedly transformed the downtown showroom and neighboring spaces into a compact audio show, bringing together manufacturers, distributors, designers and enthusiasts for a weekend centered on really listening to new gear. This year the event has added even more of a “audio show circuit” flair with the appearance of special guest, Jazz musician and composer Jacob Wendt. JW will be spinning an album recorded in all analog chain called The Gallery.

Previous get-togethers have ranged from approachable systems and personal audio to ambitious reference rigs featuring both more common and some exotic brands. More importantly, the event has offered visitors the opportunity to move between very different systems, compare approaches and speak directly with the people responsible for creating or representing the products. That exchange of ideas is one of the elements that can be difficult to reproduce during an ordinary showroom visit.

The 2024 anniversary celebration filled four listening spaces and drew an unusually deep collection of brands to San Francisco. The following year’s gathering expanded the formula again, combining carefully assembled systems with seminars, product demonstrations and appearances from industry representatives. In practical terms, these weekends have operated much like a regional audio show, only with a more focused scale and fewer hotel corridors to navigate. 2026 will revisit the 4 rooms in two locations from the anniversary edition and bring an almost equal amount of new products from a variety of brands in tow with it.

That makes the October event particularly worthwhile for anyone curious about high-end audio but reluctant to travel across the country to attend one of the larger shows. It also gives experienced listeners a chance to discover new equipment under more controlled conditions than they might encounter inside a busy convention hotel.

“Welcome to the HiFi Show” will take place Saturday, October 17th, and Sunday, October 18th, at AudioVision San Francisco. AudioVisionSF will host the world premieres of the YG Acoustics Vesper 3 loudspeakers and Nordost Norse 3 cables, giving local listeners an early opportunity to experience both introductions in a complete system. The event will also mark the U.S. premiere of the Ideon Audio NUOS Reference Streaming DAC, along with the West Coast premiere of the Ideon Audio Axiom Reference DAC, Clock and Power Supply. Other major demonstrations will include Goldmund 2800 monoblock amplifiers and the Mimesis Reference preamplifier paired with Marten Mingus Septet Statement Edition loudspeakers. Rega will make the California premiere of its Solis Reference power amplifier and Mercury Reference preamplifier, while the O Audio YMIR loudspeakers will receive their Northern California premiere. Together, these introductions turn the weekend into more than a regional gathering: attendees will have an opportunity to hear several new products before they become widely available through dealers or appear at other West Coast events. For anyone interested in where high-end audio is heading next, the concentration of premieres alone makes AudioVisionSF a particularly relevant destination this October.
