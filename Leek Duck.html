GO Pass: September

Latios soars in faster than a jet for GO Pass: September!

The GO Pass and GO Pass Deluxe for September will be available from September 8, 2026 at 10:00 am to October 6, 2026 at 10:00 am local time.

GO Pass and GO Pass Deluxe

Instaread

Trainers will automatically receive GO Pass: September on Tuesday, September 8, at 10:00 a.m. local time. You can collect GO Points and rank up to get additional rewards through Tuesday, October 6, at 10:00 a.m. local time.

Additionally, from Saturday, October 3, at 12:00 a.m. to Sunday, October 4, at 11:59 p.m. local time, there’s no daily limit for how many GO Points you can earn!

For US$7.99, Trainers can upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe, a paid version of the GO Pass that offers upgraded rewards and faster progression.

For US$9.99*, Trainers can upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe + 10 Ranks to also automatically earn enough GO Points to reach Rank 11.

While progressing through the GO Pass Deluxe, you’ll be able to claim all of the rewards from both the free GO Pass and GO Pass Deluxe. You can upgrade to a GO Pass Deluxe at any time and still collect rewards from previously unlocked ranks.

Rewards unlocked in the GO Pass will expire on Thursday, October 8, at 10:00 a.m. local time, so be sure to claim your rewards before they’re gone!

*All prices listed are in USD or the equivalent pricing tier in your local currency. Availability of event tickets and the GO Pass Deluxe may vary by region.

Featured Pokémon and Rewards

Complete Pass Tasks to rank up your GO Pass to earn the following rewards.

  • Encounter with Latios*
  • Rare Candy XL
  • Premium Battle Pass
  • Lucky Egg
  • And even more goodies!

Trainers who upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe can also earn the following rewards.

  • One Timed Incubator
  • One Super Incubator
  • Additional encounters with even more Pokémon!
  • And even more goodies!
*You might even encounter a Shiny one—if you’re lucky!

Major Milestone Bonuses

Reach Major Milestones on your GO Pass to unlock the following bonuses.

Tier 1 – Rank 1

One additional Candy for trading Pokémon
Trainers level 31 and above will receive one guaranteed Candy XL when trading Pokémon

Tier 2 – Rank 25

Open up to 40 Gifts per day
Receive up to 125 Gifts per day from spinning PokéStop and Gym Photo Discs
Hold 10 more Gifts in your Item Bag

Tier 3 – Rank 50

2× Daily Adventure Incense duration

Tier 4 – Rank 75

Increased XP and Stardust from hatching Eggs

Upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe to strengthen the following bonus:

Tier 2 – Rank 25

Open up to 50 Gifts per day
Receive up to 150 Gifts per day from spinning PokéStop and Gym Photo Discs
Hold up to 20 more Gifts in your Item Bag

The Timed Incubator

The Timed Incubator will be available on Rank 20 of GO Pass Deluxe: September!

This exciting item is an additional unlimited-use Incubator for hatching Eggs as you explore! The Timed Incubator will last from when it’s earned until one week after the current GO Pass ends.

The Timed Incubator earned as a part of GO Pass Deluxe: September will expire on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. local time, regardless of when you claimed it.*

*An Egg placed in a Timed Incubator that has not hatched by the expiration date will be placed in a new single-use Incubator to finish incubating. After the Egg hatches, the Incubator will disappear.

GO Pass

Earn points to increase your rank and unlock rewards. You can earn points by completing Pass Tasks or the various accomplishments listed here. Any rewards you earn for completing a Pass Task will be awarded as soon as you finish the task.

Trainers will also have access to Weekly Tasks to earn points and increase their rank. The Weekly Tasks are as follows:

  • Catch 75 Pokémon - 200 PTS
  • Win a raid - 150 PTS
  • Make 20 Great Throws - 150 PTS
  • Hatch 3 Eggs - 150 PTS
  • Spin 25 PokéStops or Gyms - 100 PTS
  • Use 20 Berries to help catch Pokémon - 100 PTS
  • Earn 10 hearts with your buddy - 150 PTS

There is also a daily task:

GO Pass: September Daily

  • Catch 15 Pokémon - 200 PTS

GO Pass Deluxe: September Daily

  • Catch 15 Pokémon - 200 PTS

Trainers can earn up to 250 extra points daily by completing certain tasks.

  • Win a raid - 100 PTS
  • Hatch an Egg - 40 PTS
  • Catch a Pokémon - 5 PTS

Sales

Pokémon GO Web Store

The GO Pass Deluxe, the paid version of the GO Pass, will also be available on the Pokémon GO Web Store! Your GO Pass Deluxe purchased via the Pokémon GO Web Store will activate as soon as the GO Pass begins.

Trainers who upgrade to GO Pass Deluxe: September on the Pokémon GO Web Store will receive 10 Ultra Balls, five Max Revives, one Premium Battle Pass, and five Max Potions as a gift with purchase.

Trainers who upgrade to GO Pass Deluxe: September + 10 Ranks will receive 10 Ultra Balls, five Max Revives, two Premium Battle Passes, and five Max Potions as a gift with purchase.

Or stock up on the essentials alongside your upgrade with the web-exclusive GO Pass Deluxe: September Ultra Box which includes 20 Ultra Balls, 10 Max Revives, 10 Max Potions, and five Premium Battle Passes as a gift with purchase!
