    Marriland’s Pokémon Team Builder is a tool that you can use to check the weaknesses and resistances of your entire team at a glance to spot any glaring holes in your team’s defensive coverage. It can be used both for casual or competitive purposes. It supports Pokémon Scarlet & Violet, Pokémon Legends: Z-A, as well as older generations.

    You can input up to 6 Pokémon for your team below, and you can also choose which generation to use. Pokémon unavailable in a generation will have text indicating this by the input field.

    ENEMY Team Input (Optional)

    Since you would like to check your Pokémon's move coverage against different types, you can also OPTIONALLY choose up to six ENEMY Pokémon to check your team's move's coverage against. This will factor in their weaknesses and resistances for both of their types if they have two and may help you more than just seeing the table of types. It's totally optional, though; you can leave any or all of these blank.

    Options

    Generation
    Which generation should be used for the Team Builder. In Gen. 2–5, Fairy-type is omitted; in Gen. 1, Dark- and Steel-types are omitted, and the weaknesses/resistances are slightly different. This also affects abilities and available Pokémon.
    If checked, the Team Builder will show symbols such as ◯, △, and × instead of fractions (½) or multipliers (2×).
    If checked, enables you to use Tera Types. It also adds a second type in case you want to use a Custom Type instead.
    If checked, enables you to set custom types for Pokémon. Just check this, type in "Bidoof" or something and set whatever type(s) you'd like to test.
    If checked, enables inputting up to 4 moves per Pokémon and up to six enemy Pokémon to check your Pokémon team's move coverage against (shown below main table). This will add a lot more scrolling to the page but is great for power-users!
    By pressing the Clear All button, all inputs on the page will be reset to their default. This is a good way to start from scratch without having to reload the page. A confirmation will pop up making sure you mean to do this, just in case.

    Save / Load / Share Team

    Load a Saved Team
    If you have any teams saved to your web browser, you can load them from the dropdown list here. (Loading a saved team will overwrite whatever you have inputted above.)
    Save Your Current Team
    You can save your team for later by clicking Save Team. This will save it to your browser to load later on. You can also give it a nickname to describe the team.
    Team Code
    The Team Code can be used to share teams. Copy this code to save it for later, or paste a Team Code you have in this box to load it (overwriting whatever you have inputted above).
    URL for Sharing
    Want to share your current team with friends, family, or even your future self? Copy the URL generated above and you can share it with friends by pasting it wherever you'd like (CTRL + V or Cmd + V, usually).

    Add Pokémon from List
    You can scroll through the list of Pokémon above and click or tap any of them to quickly add it to your team. Otherwise you can search for specific Pokémon below.
    Pokémon:
    Input the Pokémon you're looking for here. Suggestions will be shown as you type. You can search for its form or search for the base Pokémon and choose its form afterwards.
    Form:
    Choose which form you'd like to calculate for the Pokémon. Keep in mind that some forms, such as cosmetic-only forms, are not selectable as they do not affect the Pokémon's type.
    Ability:
    Choose an ability for the Pokémon. Only abilities that affect weakness/resistance/immunities or types are shown, as any others are irrelevant.
    Held Item:
    Choose a held item for the Pokémon. Only items that affect the Pokémon's weakness/resistance/immunities or types are shown, as any others are irrelevant.
