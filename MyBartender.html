Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini

Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini combines RumChata, cinnamon whiskey, vanilla vodka, and Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal milk in a sweet dessert cocktail with plenty of cinnamon flavor. The crushed cereal rim adds texture, while honey helps it stick and brings another layer of sweetness around the glass.

What I like about this cocktail is how closely the ingredients match the flavor of the cereal. RumChata brings cream and cinnamon, vanilla vodka adds a soft vanilla note, and the cereal milk ties everything together with that familiar toasted cereal taste.

This is a fun choice for dessert drinks, brunch gatherings, holiday parties, birthday celebrations, or any night when you want something playful and sweet. The cereal rim gives the glass a nostalgic look, while the creamy base makes it feel more like a treat than a standard martini.

Why You’ll Love This

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini is rich, sweet, and easy to recognize from the first sip.

  • It tastes like the cereal. Cinnamon, vanilla, cream, and cereal milk all point in the same direction.
  • RumChata makes it creamy. It gives the drink body and a soft cinnamon note.
  • Cinnamon whiskey adds warmth. It brings a stronger spice flavor without making the drink complicated.
  • Vanilla vodka rounds it out. The vanilla softens the sharper cinnamon notes.
  • The cereal rim adds texture. Crushed Cinnamon Toast Crunch gives every sip a little crunch.
  • It feels like dessert. This is the kind of drink that works especially well after dinner.

This cocktail is a good fit when you want something sweet, creamy, and playful without needing a long list of ingredients.

Ingredients

Each ingredient supports the cinnamon cereal theme, from the creamy liqueur to the toasted cereal garnish.

  • 2 oz RumChata
  • 1 oz cinnamon whiskey (Fireball works)
  • 1 oz vanilla vodka
  • 1 oz Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cereal Milk*
  • Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cereal, for garnish and rimming the glass 
  • Honey for rim 

*For the Cinnamont Toast Crunch Cereal Milk, soak the cereal in the milk for about 5 minutes and strain out the cereal pieces.

RumChata brings cream, cinnamon, and vanilla-like sweetness.

Cinnamon whiskey adds a stronger cinnamon note and gives the cocktail a little more heat.

Vanilla vodka softens the spice and reinforces the dessert-like flavor.

The cereal milk adds toasted cereal flavor and a little extra body.

Honey works well for the rim because it is sticky enough to hold the crushed cereal in place.

The extra cereal on top gives the drink a fun finish and makes the flavor obvious before the first sip.

Suggested Liquor Brands and Substitutions

  • RumChata: The main creamy base, with cinnamon and dairy notes that fit the cereal theme.
  • Fireball Cinnamon Whisky: A straightforward choice for adding a strong cinnamon flavor.
  • Smirnoff Vanilla Vodka: A simple option with an easy vanilla profile.
  • Absolut Vanilia: Another good vanilla vodka with a slightly cleaner finish.
  • Plain vodka: Use this if vanilla vodka is unavailable, though the drink will lose some vanilla sweetness.
  • Another cinnamon liqueur or whiskey: This can work in place of Fireball if you prefer a different spice profile.

Equipment and Glassware

This cocktail uses a few basic bar tools and a little prep for the cereal milk.

Helpful basics:

  • Martini Glass
  • Cocktail Shaker
  • Strainer
  • Small bowl
  • Small plates for honey and crushed cereal
  • Martini glass
  • Bar spoon

A martini glass works especially well because the cereal rim stays easy to see and the wide opening gives you room for garnish.

Use a fine mesh strainer for the cereal milk so small cereal pieces do not end up in the shaker.

How to Make It

The process starts with the cereal milk, then the glass is rimmed before the cocktail is shaken.

Prepare the cereal milk by adding ½ cup of Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal to a bowl and pouring the milk over it. Let steep for 20–30 minutes while you prepare the rest of the cocktail.

Crush the remaining Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal into a fine crumb for the rim.

Pour the honey onto a small plate. Dip the rim of a martini glass into the honey, then coat it with the crushed cereal.

Add the RumChata, vanilla vodka, and cinnamon whiskey to a shaker filled with ice.

Strain the cereal milk into the shaker and shake well until chilled.

Strain the cocktail into the prepared martini glass.

Garnish with a few pieces of Cinnamon Toast Crunch.

The cereal milk and crushed rim are what give this drink most of its signature character, so take a little extra care with those two steps.

Pro Tips

A few small details can make the Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini cleaner, smoother, and easier to serve.

  • Crush the cereal finely for the rim. Large pieces do not stick as evenly.
  • Use only a thin layer of honey. Too much can make the rim messy and overly sweet.
  • Strain the cereal milk well. This helps keep the cocktail smooth.
  • Chill the martini glass first. A cold glass helps the creamy drink stay colder longer.
  • Shake firmly with ice. Creamy ingredients benefit from a good shake.
  • Do not overfill the rim with cereal. A thinner coating is easier to sip.
  • Add the garnish right before serving. The cereal can soften if it sits in the drink too long.
  • Serve immediately. The cocktail is best while cold and the rim is still crisp.

If you want the cereal flavor to come through more strongly, use fresh cereal when making the milk and avoid letting the garnish sit in moisture before serving.

Variations

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini is easy to customize while keeping the same dessert-cocktail idea.

  • Chocolate Version: Add a small amount of chocolate liqueur for a cinnamon-chocolate combination.
  • Coffee Version: Add a splash of chilled espresso for a breakfast-inspired twist.
  • Bourbon Version: Replace the cinnamon whiskey with bourbon for a warmer, less candy-like profile.
  • Extra Creamy Version: Use whole milk or a little half and half in the cereal milk.
  • Non-Alcoholic Version: Use cereal milk, vanilla syrup, cinnamon syrup, and a splash of cream with the same cereal rim.

You can also dust the top lightly with ground cinnamon if you want another layer of aroma.

When to Serve and Pairings

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini works best when you want something sweet and dessert-like.

It is a good choice for:

  • Brunch gatherings
  • Holiday parties
  • Birthday celebrations
  • Dessert cocktail nights
  • Weekend get-togethers
  • After-dinner drinks

For food, pair it with sweet or salty snacks that do not overpower the cinnamon.

Try it with:

  • Sugar cookies
  • Churros
  • Cinnamon rolls
  • Vanilla cupcakes
  • Salted pretzels
  • Cheesecake
  • Caramel popcorn

It also works well next to drinks such as a Pumpkin Spiced Espresso Martini, The Ghosted Martini, or Devil’s Kiss.

I especially like this one after dinner because it already feels close to dessert, so you do not need much alongside it.

Storage and Serving Notes

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini is best shaken and served fresh.

You can prepare the cereal milk ahead of time and keep it refrigerated in a covered container.

For the freshest flavor and texture, use the cereal milk within a day.

Do not rim the glass too far ahead because the cereal can soften once it touches honey.

The spirit mixture can also be pre-measured and chilled, then shaken with cereal milk and ice just before serving.

If you are making several drinks, prepare the cereal milk and crushed cereal in advance, then rim and shake each drink close to serving time.

FAQs

Here are a few common questions about the Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini.

What does a Cinnamon Toast Crunch Martini taste like?

It tastes creamy, sweet, cinnamon-forward, and vanilla-heavy, with a toasted cereal note from the infused milk.

Can I make the cereal milk ahead of time?

Yes. Keep it refrigerated in a covered container and use it while fresh.

Can I use regular vodka instead of vanilla vodka?

Yes. The drink will be slightly less sweet and less vanilla-forward.

Can I use another cinnamon whiskey instead of Fireball?

Yes. Any cinnamon whiskey with a flavor you enjoy can work.

Can I skip the cereal rim?

Yes, though the rim is a big part of the drink’s flavor and presentation.

Can I make this cocktail less sweet?

You can choose a less sweet cinnamon whiskey or use plain vodka in place of vanilla vodka in a future version.
