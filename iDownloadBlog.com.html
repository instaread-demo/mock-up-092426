Download five minimalist wallpapers featuring a sculptural glass ribbon in blue, rust, teal, purple, and ink, designed to look clean and elegant on your iPhone or Android phone’s Lock Screen and Home Screen.

Glass ribbon wallpaper

I watch a lot of glasswork videos, especially from a channel called 2BGlass, where they make cups, glasses, Christmas ornaments, Halloween pumpkins, etc., using hot liquid glass. I find the process of giving shape to molten flowing glass fascinating, and that’s what first came to my mind when I saw these ribbon wallpapers on my X timeline posted by prolific digital creator @aan__x. Since X doesn’t always maintain the best media quality, I sourced these images from ᴀɴ ᴡᴀʟʟᴘᴀᴘᴇʀꜱ’s Telegram account that goes by @aanwallpapers.

At the center of these wallpapers is a smooth, curved ribbon design which keeps things minimal while adding a refined, sophisticated touch. The soft pastel colors are pleasing to the eyes and don’t overshadow the on-screen elements like the clock, widgets, app icons, buttons, etc.

Out of the five wallpapers, the ink version is my favorite, but I’m sure you can’t go wrong with either. Or just try them all on both the Lock and Home Screens, and let us know which one you prefer.
