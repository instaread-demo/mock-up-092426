Priscilla Ojo on women shooting shots at her husband: His choice, not theirs

· May 4, 2026

Priscilla Ojo-Mkambala just said something real about marriage, celebrity, and the whole circus that comes with dating someone famous. During a podcast interview, she addressed the fact that women openly flirt with her husband, and yeah, some have even said they wouldn't mind being a second wife. She wasn't angry about it. She was just honest.

Her brother apparently wasn't as chill. He disapproved hard. But Priscilla saw it differently. The women making moves? Not really the issue for her. It's about him. "If he decides to go that route, it's his decision to make," she said, and that's the whole thing right there.

She gets it. Being married to an artist means living in the spotlight. The attention is real, the admiration is extreme sometimes, and fans can be… a lot. That's just the life. But none of that matters if your partner has discipline and integrity. Those are the things that actually count.

What was interesting was how she didn't blame the women throwing themselves at her husband or act like she's some victim of it all. Instead, she put the responsibility exactly where it belongs: on him. On his choices. On what he decides to do when the attention is constant. That's either refreshing or wild depending on how you look at marriage, but it's definitely confidence. She trusts him, or at least that's what she's saying. And whether that trust is warranted? That's between them.

The bigger thing though is that she's not pretending this isn't part of the deal. Being married to someone in the entertainment industry means sharing them in a weird way. You share their image, their time, their energy. Other people feel entitled to a piece of them. And yeah, sometimes those people cross lines. But Priscilla's saying the line that actually matters is the one your partner chooses to draw.
